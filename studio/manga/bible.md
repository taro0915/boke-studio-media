# Series bible — "Second Bloom" (working title)

Love stories from Japan, for women 45+. One episode = one complete story told in one Instagram carousel (cover + 8 panels + end card). Rarely a two-part story (part 2 posted the next night).

## Who reads it
Women 45–64 in the US and India. They have raised kids, built careers, survived marriages or divorces, lost parents or spouses. They want to feel that love is still possible for them, and they want to cry a little and smile at the end.

## Tone
- K-drama emotional beats: fated reunion, umbrella in the rain, hand catching a wrist, a small object that carries meaning, a slow burn that pays off on the last panel.
- Grown-up, warm, dignified. Gentle humor allowed. Never cynical, never mocking either partner.
- Every story ends hopeful or tender. Bittersweet is fine, hopeless is not.

## Rules
- Main characters are Japanese, age 45–65. Setting is Japan (train platforms, shotengai shopping streets, onsen towns, rainy Tokyo, Kyoto backstreets, seaside towns in Okinawa, izakaya, bookstores, hospitals, school reunions, flower shops, festivals, snow in Hokkaido).
- The worries are universal: empty nest, a husband who stopped noticing, divorce and starting over, widowhood, caring for an aging parent, retirement, first love reunited, regret, a second chance.
- Clean romance: hand-holding, a hug, at most a gentle kiss. No sexual content, no violence, no real people, no brand names, no famous characters.
- No cheating romanticized. If someone is married, the story is about rekindling with their own spouse.
- English is simple and short. Each panel: at most 1 narration line and at most 2 speech lines, each under 14 words.
- Japanese words are welcome when the meaning is obvious from context (e.g. "Okaeri." — "Welcome home.").

## Story patterns (rotate, data will decide which to repeat)
1. First love reunited after 30 years
2. The husband who quietly remembered everything (rekindling a long marriage)
3. Widow/widower and the neighbor who shows up every day
4. Divorced at 50, the first date in 25 years
5. The regular customer at a small shop
6. Caring for a parent, meeting someone in the hospital corridor
7. Retirement trip alone that turns into a meeting
8. An old letter / old photo that brings two people back
9. Class reunion: the one who never said it
10. Grown children set their parent up (comedy + heart)
